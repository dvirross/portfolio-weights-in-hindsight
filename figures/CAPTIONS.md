## 01-two-asset-hill

- **Caption:** With monthly rebalancing, every split between two industries has one growth rate, and the curve has a single peak. ## 08-sudden-events

- **Caption:** A sudden crash hits a rebalanced portfolio as hard as any other: on 19 October 1987 the equal-weight 3× portfolio lost about half its value in one day, with or without monthly rebalancing. Rebalancing acts only afterwards, by buying what fell; in 2020 that helped less than holding the winners (1.24 against 1.62 by the end of the year).
- **Alt text:** Two line charts. Left, August 1987 to December 1988: both 3× portfolios fall from 1 to about 0.28 in October 1987 and end near 0.50; the unleveraged portfolio falls to about 0.72. Right, 2020: the 3× portfolios fall to about 0.24 in March and end at 1.24 (rebalanced) and 1.62 (never rebalanced).
- **Source line:** Kenneth R. French Data Library, 10 value-weighted US industry portfolios, daily returns 1926–2026 (including dividends). 3× series built from them: 3 × daily return − 2 × risk-free rate − 0.95% a year, reset daily. Daily simulation, rebalancing at month ends, no costs.
